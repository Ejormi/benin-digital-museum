Motivation

As a self-taught web developer with a strong interest in digital humanities, I created this museum to explore how technology can preserve and share cultural heritage.

Introduction

Welcome to the Benin Digital Art Museum — a virtual space dedicated to celebrating the artistry, history, and legacy of the Benin Bronzes. These remarkable works of art, crafted by the Edo people of Nigeria, represent centuries of cultural expression and craftsmanship. Our mission is to make this heritage accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Mission Statement

  • Showcase the beauty and complexity of the Benin Bronzes
  • Educate global audiences about their historical significance
  • Promote dialogue around restitution, ownership, and cultural memory

Why Digital?

  • Reduce barriers to access for students, researchers, and enthusiasts
  • Provide interactive experiences that enhance learning
  • Ensure long-term preservation through modern technology

Behind the Scenes

  • Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for a clean and responsive interface
  • Hosted on Netlify for fast and reliable performance
  • Accessibility-first design to ensure inclusivity for all users

Cultural Context

The Benin Art are more than art — they are historical documents. Each piece tells a story of royal lineage, spiritual beliefs, and societal structure. Many were taken during the British Punitive Expedition of 1897 and now reside in museums around the world. This digital space seeks to honor their origins and spark conversations about their future.
